About Prime Vector Private Limited

Building Next-Gen Tech Talent for the Global Industry

Prime Vector Private Limited (primevector.in) is a premier EdTech company headquartered in Hosur, Tamil Nadu. We specialize in skill development, industry internships, corporate training, and campus placement drives.

Our state-of-the-art LMS connects students, educators, and enterprise hiring teams to deliver job-ready technical mastery in AI, Data Science, Cloud, IoT, and Software Engineering.

🎯 Our Mission

To transform academic learning into practical industry capability by providing high-impact skill training, real project experiences, and seamless career placements for every ambitious learner.
